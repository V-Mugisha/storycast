About & Accessibility

Learn about our storytelling mission and the core accessibility standards built into every StoryCast production.

Our mission

StoryCast is a small library of stories told in sound, image, and voice. Our first story is Noah and the Ark, retold in our own words with the old verses close at hand. We believe a story is only truly told when everyone can reach it, so accessibility is built into the site from the start, not added at the end.

Accessibility features

  • Captions on every video, provided through the captions track.
  • A full, visible transcript for every audio and video story.
  • Semantic landmarks: header, main, article, section, aside, and footer.
  • A clear heading hierarchy with a single h1 on each page.
  • A skip-to-content link and fully keyboard operable navigation.
  • Visible focus indicators on all links and buttons.
  • Text and interface colors that meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast.
  • Respect for the prefers-reduced-motion setting.
  • Descriptive alt text on images, with decorative images marked as empty.

How to engage

There are a few ways to take part in a story.

  • Watch: play the video and turn captions on with the controls.
  • Listen: play the audio and read along with the transcript.
  • Read: the full text of every story lives in its transcript section.
  • Contribute: have a story to suggest or an issue to report? Write to us at m.victor@alustudent.com.
